9 · What STRATA proposes beyond standard practice

Reviewed: SPE-227235-MS (real-time analytics, ILT KPIs, rig benchmarking) and SPE-229231-MS (tree-ensemble instability risk, parameter recommendations). Both describe established industry practice and publish no model metrics. STRATA competes on what they leave out: reproducibility, measured disagreement, honest validation and verified AI answers.
