Known limitations
  • Surface data only. No downhole sensors; anything downhole is inferred.
  • 1 Hz sampling. High-frequency vibration (stick-slip, whirl) is not visible.
  • Geothermal vs hydrocarbon. FORGE granite wells and Volve sandstone wells are not like-for-like benchmarks.
  • Daily reports are coarse. 15–30 min rows; some mismatches are granularity, not error.
  • Advisory only. No setpoints, no control, no fleet-scale claims.
